👋 Introduction

Welcome to Find The Differences Five Diff ("the Game"), developed by Tech4App. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you play our game. By playing the Game, you agree to this policy.

📦 Information We Collect

We do not collect personally identifiable information directly. However, the following data is collected automatically through third-party services:

  • Device information: Device model, OS version, language, screen resolution
  • Gameplay analytics: Levels played, completion status, stars earned, hints used
  • Crash reports: Diagnostic data when the app encounters an error
  • Advertising ID: Used to serve personalized or non-personalized ads
  • Local storage: Game progress, hint count, and preferences stored on your device only

    Google Payments Privacy Notice

📢 Advertising

The Game shows banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. If you are located in the EU/EEA/UK, you will be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown. You may choose non-personalized ads at any time through the consent dialog.

To opt out of personalized ads on Android, go to:
Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization

💳 In-App Purchases

The Game offers optional purchases (hints packs and subscriptions) through Google Play. We do not store any payment or billing information. All transactions are handled by Google Play's secure payment system.

👶 Children's Privacy

The Game is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

🔐 Data Security

Game progress and preferences are stored locally on your device using Unity PlayerPrefs and are not transmitted to our servers. All data collected by third-party SDKs is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

🌍 Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data collected about you
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of analytics collection (by uninstalling the app)
  • Withdraw ad personalization consent (via in-app consent dialog)

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the Game after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the new policy.
